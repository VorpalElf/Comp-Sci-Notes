## Boolean Expression Syntax
|               | Sum of Products                        | Product of Sums                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Combine       | OR                                     | AND                                  |
| Smaller Terms | AND                                    | OR                                   |
| Example       | $(a \land b \land c) \lor (d \land e)$ | $(a \lor b \lor c) \land (d \lor e)$ |

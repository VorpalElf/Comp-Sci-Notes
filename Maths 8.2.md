## Introduction
$$\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{k! \times (n-k)!}$$
Number of ways to pick $k$ elements out of the set $n$

| Task                                  | Formula | Order Matters? |
| ------------------------------------- | ------- | -------------- |
| **Arrange** $k$ of $n$ distinct items | P(n, k) | ✅              |
| **Choose** $k$ of $n$ distinct items  | C(n, k) | ❌              |
## Binomial Theorem
$$(x+y)^n = \sum^n_{k=0} \binom n k x^{n-k}y^k$$
#### Pascal's Identity
$$\binom n k = \binom {n-1} {k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}$$

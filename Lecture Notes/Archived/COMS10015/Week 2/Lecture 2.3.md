## Introduction
![[Screenshot 2026-09-21 at 11.43.37 PM.png|373]]
- Atom made out of protons, neutrons and electrons
- $n$-th shell can accommodate up to $2n^2$ electrons
- An unfilled slot is called a hole

### Why Silicon?
1. Abundant
2. More electrons configuration with donor materials
3. Inert (i.e. stable)

## Conductors & Transistors
⭐️ P-Type: extra holes (i.e. positive)
⭐️ N-Type: extra electrons (i.e. negative)

### Vacuum Tube
![[Screenshot 2026-09-23 at 10.28.48 AM.png|131]]
- Glass envelope holding vacuum
- Produce electron by thermionic emission
- Used to fail during start-ups & shut down

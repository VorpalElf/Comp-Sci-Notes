## Introduction
Binary relation: Subset of A and B
## Properties
- Reflective: Every element is related to itself
- Symmetric: Whenever $x$ is related to $y$, the reverse relation also holds
- Antisymmetric: ’two-way’ related elements must in fact be the same
- Transitive: If $x$ related to $y$ and $y$ related to $z$, $x$ is related to $z$
## Partial & Total Order
### Partial Order
1. Reflexive
2. Antisymmetric (i.e. not symmetric)
3. Transitive
### Total Order
- Needs to be a partial order
- Every pair of distinct elements is comparable
## Composition of Relations
### Powers
- $R^0$ is the identity relation
- $R^1 = R$
- $R^2 = R \circ R$
- $R^{n+1} = R \circ R^n$
